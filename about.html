( 01 ) — About the label

Beneath

the surface, something is always looking back.

SUBSTRATA is a one-person poster label founded in Kobe in 2026 by designer Zaw Paing. It started as a dare — five posters in a single day — and became an archive of distorted bodies, watching eyes and burning light.

( 03 ) — Method

How an image goes underground

Every poster passes through the same four rooms. None of them are clean.

I

Source

Found photographs, anatomical scans, a dog on a white background. Raw material only.

II

Distort

Cling film, x-ray, heat maps, chromatic tearing. The body stops being comfortable.

III

Typeset

Condensed capitals, gothic script, medical data logs. Type is treated as evidence.

IV

Print

Pigment on 308gsm cotton rag. Fifty at most. Signed, numbered, never again.
